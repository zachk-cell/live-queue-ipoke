Privacy Policy — Poke Pig Live Queue

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Poke Pig Live Queue ("the app") is a private, self-use order-management tool operated by the seller to organize and fulfill orders received through their own TikTok Shop during live sales. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information we access

When connected to the seller's TikTok Shop via the official TikTok Shop API, the app receives order information for that shop, which may include the order number, buyer username, ordered items and quantities, order total, and order status. The app does not request or store buyer shipping addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or payment details beyond what is required to display and manage the fulfillment queue.

How we use it

Order information is used solely to build a live, ordered fulfillment queue for the seller and their staff, and to show buyers their approximate position in line. The public-facing view displays only a buyer's username and queue position; order totals and any personal details are never shown publicly.

How we store and share it

Order data is held only as long as needed to operate the queue for the current and recent sales, and is used only by the seller's own shop. We do not sell, rent, or share order or buyer information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising. Access to the control panel is password-protected.

Data removal

The seller can clear stored queue data at any time from within the app. Buyers who wish to have their information removed, or who have questions about this policy, can contact us at the address below.
